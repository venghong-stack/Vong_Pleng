About Vong Pleng
Stream the Sound of Cambodia

Vong Pleng is a music streaming platform that provides
a wide range of music content, including local and international music,
podcasts, and live radio. Our mission is to connect people with the music
they love and to support artists in sharing their work with the world.
We are committed to providing a high-quality listening experience
for our users and to promoting the rich musical culture of Cambodia.
